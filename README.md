# Favorite Movie: Fight Club (1999)

**Fight Club** is a 1999 American psychological thriller film directed by *David Fincher* and based on the 1996 novel of the same name by *Chuck Palahniuk*.

> "The first rule of Fight Club is: you do not talk about Fight Club."

## Synopsis
An insomniac office worker and a devil-may-care soap maker form an underground fight club that evolves into much more, challenging consumerist culture and societal norms.

## Main Cast & Characters
* **Edward Norton** as The Narrator
* **Brad Pitt** as Tyler Durden
* **Helena Bonham Carter** as Marla Singer
* **Meat Loaf** as Robert "Bob" Paulson
